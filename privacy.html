Privacy policy

Shop Follow-ups · Last updated 2 November 2026

Shop Follow-ups is a private tool used by one family business to follow up with people who asked about buying one of its shops. This page explains what data it uses and how.

Data from connected Gmail accounts

  • The tool asks Google only for permission to send email (gmail.send) and to know the connected email address.
  • It cannot read, search, change or delete any email in the connected account.
  • The permission token Google returns is stored privately in the owner's Google Apps Script account. It is never shown on any web page, never written to a spreadsheet, and never shared with anyone.
  • Google user data is used only to send the follow-up emails the family asks the tool to send. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.

Contact lists

Names, emails, phone numbers and consent details of interested buyers are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the family. Only invited family members can see it. People are only messaged if they agreed to be contacted, and anyone who asks to stop is marked as opted out and never messaged again.

Removing access or data

  • A connected Gmail account can be disconnected at any time in the tool's Settings, or by the account owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your contact details removed, email the address below and they will be deleted.
