Shop Follow-ups

A private tool used by one family business in the United Arab Emirates.

Shop Follow-ups helps the owners of a few shops follow up with people who asked about buying a shop. A family member uploads the list of interested people, reviews a message, and the tool sends a personal follow-up email from that shop's own Gmail account, one email every few minutes. It can also open WhatsApp with a ready-written message that a person sends by hand.

Who can use it

Only family members invited by the owner. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Google permissions

When a shop's Gmail account is connected, the tool asks Google for one Gmail permission only: send email on your behalf (gmail.send). It cannot read, search, change or delete any email. It also reads the account's email address so it knows which account was connected.
