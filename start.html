How to start

For anyone who wants to use Shop Follow-ups. About 15 minutes the first time. Free.

Open Shop Follow-ups

App link (to copy and send to others): https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbxbNPWgRTi4oL_4c0Z_jNo2FH-zCCc7M20dGO-TMZkIkq5vpFTXS2kv1RzDlk4K8S4/exec

Before you start: use your own computer, or your own Chrome profile (Chrome → your picture at the top right → Add). Google always opens this app as the first Google account signed in to the browser. If someone else is signed in first, you would open their workspace. To try another account quickly, use a private window: ⌘ + Shift + N (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + N (Windows).

1. Open the app (first time only)

  1. Click Open Shop Follow-ups above and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Google shows “Google hasn’t verified this app”. This is normal for this small free app. Click Advanced → Go to Shop Follow-ups (unsafe).
  3. Click Allow. The app may create your Google Sheet, connect to Gmail/WhatsApp services, and send in the background. It does not get access to your email inbox.

2. Create your workspace

  1. On the Welcome page, check the email shown is yours (if not, see the tip above).
  2. Write a workspace name (for example “My shops”) and your first shop’s name.
  3. Click Create my workspace. A new Google Sheet is made in your Google Drive. Only you can see it.

Someone already uses the app with you (family, a partner)? Don’t create a workspace: ask them to add your Gmail in their ⚙ Settings → People, then reload the page.

3. Connect the Gmail that sends

  1. Click ⚙ Settings (top right).
  2. Under Gmail accounts, click + Connect a Gmail account. A Google tab opens.
  3. Choose the Gmail that should send for your shop (it can be different from your own account).
  4. “Google hasn’t verified this app” → Advanced → Go to Shop Follow-ups (unsafe).
  5. Tick Send email on your behalf → Continue. Come back to the app tab: it shows ✓ Connected.
  6. Click Send test next to it and check that Gmail’s inbox got the test email.
  7. Repeat for every Gmail you want to send from. You can connect as many as you need.

4. Set up your shops

  1. In ⚙ Settings → Shops, choose for each shop which connected Gmail sends its emails. It saves at once and shows ✓ Ready.
  2. More shops: on the home page click + Add a shop, write the name and click Add, then choose its Gmail in ⚙ Settings → Shops.
  3. Change a shop’s name: open the shop and click ✎ Rename next to its name, type the new name, click Save.
  4. Optional: People → write a helper’s Gmail → Add. They open the same link and can upload and send, but cannot change Settings.

5. Upload the people to contact

  1. Click ← All shops and choose a shop.
  2. On 1 Contacts, drag your Excel file onto the box (or click Choose file).
  3. Check each column choice: Name, Email, Phone, and Consent (did they agree to be contacted?). Click Import.
  4. Only people with a clear “yes” consent get messages. If your file has no consent column but everyone agreed, tick Everyone in this file agreed… before importing.

6. Send the emails

  1. Open 2 Send emails. Write the subject and message. Click + Insert the person’s name so each person gets “Hello Sara,”, “Hello Ahmed,”…
  2. Choose when to start and how many minutes between emails (5 is safe).
  3. Click Check who will get it →: it shows who gets it, who is skipped and why, and exactly what they see.
  4. Click Start sending. You can close the page and turn off the laptop: the emails go out by themselves.
  5. Follow it on 3 Progress. You can Pause, Resume or Stop for good any time.

7. WhatsApp (optional)

On a shop’s WhatsApp tab, choose one:

  • From a Mac (free): the workspace owner clicks Set up a Mac, copies the line shown, and on the Mac opens Terminal (⌘ + Space, type Terminal, Enter), pastes it (⌘ + V) and presses Enter. After “Done”, click Open WhatsApp on the Mac and scan the QR code with the phone (WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices). The Mac must stay on while sending.
  • WhatsApp Business API (paid to Meta): works even with every laptop off. The owner connects it once in ⚙ Settings → WhatsApp Business API (the steps are written there). Then choose an approved template and press Start sending.
  • By hand: click Open WhatsApp next to a person and press Send yourself.

If something goes wrong

  • I see someone else’s email or shops: another Google account is first in this browser. Use your own Chrome profile or a private window (tip at the top). Click Not you? in the app for help.
  • “Access blocked” or “This app is blocked”: the app has reached Google’s limit of 100 accounts for unverified apps. Contact the address below.
  • A shop says “Sending Gmail not connected”: ⚙ Settings → connect the Gmail, then choose it under Shops.
  • A send is paused with “needs to be reconnected”: ⚙ Settings → Reconnect next to that Gmail → Allow → then Resume on the Progress tab.
  • Someone asks to stop: find them on Contacts and click Mark opted out. They are never messaged again.
